Questions

Good questions to ask

Does Solvere use cameras?

No, never. Radar sensors detect movement and breathing, not images. Nobody can watch your parent.

Do they need to wear or charge anything?

No. The pods and hub plug into outlets that are already there and keep a battery for power outages. Tags run on a small coin cell and report low battery early. In a bathroom with no outlet, the Room pod runs on its battery and the app tells the family well before it needs a charge. If your parent already wears a smartwatch, Solvere can use it too.

Who comes when help is needed?

Your parent is asked first. If they need help, the nearest person on their list is sent: building staff, a Solvere caregiver where we operate, or a family member you choose. Emergency services only when needed.
