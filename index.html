Questions

Is there a camera in every room?

No. The Cam goes only in shared rooms your parent agrees to, like the living room. Bedrooms and bathrooms use Solvere Sense, which has no camera at all.

What happens if they fall?

Solvere asks them out loud if they're okay. If they say they're fine, that's it. If there's no answer, you get an alert straight away with what happened and when.

Do they need to wear or charge anything?

No. Nothing to wear, nothing to remember, no button to press.

Can I buy Solvere today?

Not yet. We have a working prototype and are choosing our first pilot homes. Join the waitlist and we'll be in touch.

Is it for care homes too?

Yes. Choose “Care home” when you join and we'll talk about a pilot.
