Questions

Good questions to ask

Does Solvere use cameras?

Only in shared rooms your parent agrees to, like the living room. Never in bedrooms or bathrooms. There, Solvere Sense uses radar and motion sensors with no image at all.

What happens after a possible fall?

Solvere asks your parent out loud if they're okay. If they say they're fine, that's the end of it. If there's no answer, the family gets a message with what happened, the time and the reason.

Do they need to wear or charge anything?

No. Nothing to wear, nothing to remember, no button to press.

Is Solvere available now?

Not yet. We have a working prototype and are choosing our first pilot homes. Join the waitlist and we'll be in touch.

Where will Solvere be available?

We're starting with a small pilot. Join from anywhere, and we'll tell you when Solvere reaches your area.

Is it for care homes too?

Yes. Choose “Care home” when you join and we'll talk about a pilot for your residents.
