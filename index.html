How it works

It notices. It asks. It tells you.

Notices

Every second, Solvere works out whether your parent is standing, sitting, walking, resting, or may have fallen.

Asks

After a possible fall, it asks out loud: “Are you okay?” If they say they're fine, nobody gets a scare.

Tells you

No answer? The family gets a message straight away: what happened, when, and why Solvere thinks it was a fall.

Privacy

Their home. Their rules.

Your parent decides where Solvere can look. Private rooms stay private.

Living room · kitchen

A camera, only with their yes

  • Only in shared rooms your parent agrees to.
  • Stick-figure mode shows body position, not their face.
  • Solvere reads body position on a device in the home, so most of what it needs never leaves the room.

Bedroom · bathroom

No camera, ever

  • Small radar and motion sensors instead of video.
  • Notices when someone stops moving for too long.
  • Nothing in these rooms can show an image of a person.

Questions

Good questions to ask

Does Solvere use cameras?

Only in shared rooms your parent agrees to, like the living room. Never in bedrooms or bathrooms. There, small radar and motion sensors notice movement without any image.

What happens after a possible fall?

Solvere asks your parent out loud if they're okay. If they say they're fine, that's the end of it. If there's no answer, the family gets a message with what happened, the time and the reason.

Do they need to wear or charge anything?

No. Nothing to wear, nothing to remember, no button to press.
