What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, email address and whether you are joining as a family member, care home, older adult or other.

Why

Only to contact you about Solvere: pilot places, launch news and short questions about your needs.

Who sees it

Only the Solvere team. We don't sell or share your details. Waitlist sign-ups are received and stored for us by our form provider, Formspree.

Your choices

Ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing hello@trysolvere.com. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.
